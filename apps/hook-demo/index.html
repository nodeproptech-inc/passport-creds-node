Demo script (2 minutes)
  1. Rui swaps — identity with no claims → pool reverts NotCompliant(MISSING_KYC).
  2. ⚡ Verify Rui's KYC (the issuer signs EIP-712, Rui submits it to his own Identity) → he trades the Deal Room pool.
  3. Ana tries the Investor pool → MISSING_ACCREDITED. Verify accreditation → unlocked.
  4. Ana adds liquidity (watch her LP position and token deltas), then Revoke KYC ⚠ (the issuer's latch) → both pools refuse her — but Exit still works. Never trap funds. Restore KYC puts her back in.
  5. ⏩ Fast-forward 1 year — claims expire; even the operator gets MISSING_KYC. Compliance is live, not a snapshot.
